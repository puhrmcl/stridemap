What it does

Map-first

A full-screen map is the whole app. Runs, rides, hikes and walks, together or one at a time.

From Apple Health

Apple Watch, Nike, Garmin, COROS, Strava — anything that saves workouts to Health. Or import GPX, TCX and FIT files.

Explore & recap

Cities, states, parks and records, plus an animated Year in Review.

Etch Studio

Turn a route, a year, or every city you've reached into a print on museum-grade paper.

Private by design

Your activities stay on your device. No account, nothing syncs. Order a print and only that artwork and your address are sent, so it can be made and posted.

Common questions

Why is my map empty? Etch draws activities from Apple Health. Grant the Health permission on first launch, and make sure you have workouts recorded with GPS routes. New activities appear automatically as you record them.

Do I need Strava? No. Strava is optional and only adds extra details (titles, gear, races) to runs already in Apple Health.

How do I delete my data? Use Delete Cache in Settings, or delete the app — everything is stored on your device.
