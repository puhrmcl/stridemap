Common questions

Why is my map empty? Etch draws runs from Apple Health. Grant the Health permission on first launch, and make sure you have running workouts (with GPS routes) recorded. New runs appear automatically as you record them.

Do I need Strava? No. Strava is optional and only adds extra details (titles, gear, races) to runs already in Apple Health.

How do I delete my data? Use Delete Cache in Settings, or delete the app — everything is stored on your device.
