Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

The short version. Etch keeps your running history on your device. We do not operate servers, we do not have accounts, and we never collect, sell, or share your data. Your Apple Health and location data are used only on your iPhone to draw your runs on the map.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Etch app (“Etch,” “the app,” “we,” “us”) handles your information. Etch is a map-first running app that visualizes the runs in your Apple Health history. By using Etch you agree to this policy.

1. Information the app accesses

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Etch reads running workouts and their associated data from Apple Health so it can display them on a map. This may include:

  • Running workouts and their dates and times
  • Workout routes (GPS coordinates)
  • Distance and duration
  • Elevation gain
  • Heart rate
  • Active energy (calories)
  • Cadence, when available

This access is read-only — Etch never writes to Apple Health. Health data read by the app is stored only on your device.

Location

Etch may show your current position on the map to give your runs context. Your live location is used only for on-screen display and is not recorded or transmitted by the app.

Strava (optional)

Connecting Strava is entirely optional. If you choose to connect it, Etch uses Strava’s official OAuth sign-in and requests read-only access to your activities in order to enrich your runs with information Apple Health may not provide — such as activity titles, gear (shoes), descriptions, and race identification. Your Strava access token is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device, and the enrichment data is cached locally alongside your other runs. You can disconnect Strava at any time in Settings, which removes the stored token.

2. How your information is used

All information is used for a single purpose: to display your runs and running statistics inside the app, on your device. Processing happens locally on your iPhone.

Our commitments regarding Health data.

  • We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not share Apple Health data with any third parties.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not store Apple Health data in iCloud or on any server.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose other than the app’s core functionality of showing you your runs.

3. Where your data is stored

Etch stores your runs locally on your device using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData). Authentication tokens (for Strava, if connected) are kept in the iOS Keychain. Etch does not run its own servers and does not upload your data anywhere.

4. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information. Because Etch has no backend, your data is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the app. The only time data leaves your device is when you explicitly choose to export it — for example, using the GPX export feature or the system share sheet — in which case you control where it goes.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple Health & Apple Maps. Etch uses Apple frameworks (HealthKit and MapKit) that run on your device and are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Strava (only if you connect it). When you connect Strava, your use of Strava is subject to Strava’s Privacy Policy. Etch requests read-only access and does not post to or modify your Strava account.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Health access. You can review or revoke Etch’s access to Apple Health at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Etch (or in the Health app).
  • Location. You can manage location access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • Strava. You can disconnect Strava in the app’s Settings, and you can revoke Etch’s access from your Strava account settings.
  • Delete your data. Use Delete Cache in the app’s Settings to remove all stored runs from the device. Deleting the app removes all of its local data.

7. Data security

Your data stays on your device, protected by iOS. Sensitive credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping data on-device and avoiding servers removes entire categories of risk.

8. Children’s privacy

Etch is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be reflected in an updated version of the app or on this page.
