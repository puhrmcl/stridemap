# Verde House — Identity System

A refinement of the existing prickly-pear identity. The concept is unchanged: three pads,
an extremely minimal silhouette, deep Verde green, a stacked VERDE / HOUSE wordmark.
Everything below is drawn from geometry, not typed — every mark in `svg/` is generated by
`tools/build_identity.py` and can be rebuilt exactly.

    python3 tools/identity.py     # or: python3 -c "import identity; identity.emit()"

## The unit

All measurements are expressed in **cap-height units**: the wordmark's cap height = 100.
This is the only ruler in the system; it survives any output size.

## 1 · The symbol

One canonical pad, drawn as **four mutually tangent circular arcs** — a top cap circle
(R 29), a base cap circle (R 17.5) and two flank arcs (R ≈ 68) tangent to both. Nothing is
freehand. Pad proportion 0.66 w/h, widest point 46% down from the apex.

That single pad is instantiated three times:

| pad         | linear scale | area share | rotation |
|-------------|--------------|-----------|----------|
| main        | 1.000        | 50.0 %    | −6°      |
| upper right | 0.7862       | 30.9 %    | +34°     |
| left        | 0.6180       | 19.1 %    | −27°     |

The scale ratio is **1/√φ = 0.7862**, so each pad's *area* is exactly φ times the next
(1 : 0.618 : 0.382). The pads are individually symmetric; all of the organic character
comes from the composition, not from distorting the shape.

Junction gaps are identical on both joints — **1.56 % of symbol height** — solved
numerically rather than eyeballed. Symbol proportion **0.900 w/h**. Centre of ink at
(0.527 w, 0.479 h); use that point, not the bounding box, to centre the mark in a
square container.

### Micro cut

`symbol-micro.svg` widens both junctions to **2.55 % of height** so the three pads stay
separate below about 32 px, in embroidery, and in deboss. Use it under 32 px / 12 mm.
Below **20 px**, use the VH monogram instead — three pads cannot resolve at 16 px and
pretending otherwise produces a blob.

## 2 · The wordmark

Eight capitals — V E R D H O U S — drawn for this identity. Not a licensed font; there is
nothing to license, embed or accidentally update.

| | value | as % of cap |
|---|---|---|
| cap height | 100 | — |
| stem | 13.2 | 13.2 % |
| horizontal bar | 11.9 | 0.901 × stem |
| round side stroke | 13.6 | 1.030 × stem |
| round top/bottom | 11.6 | 0.853 × side |
| overshoot | 1.3 | 1.3 % per side |

Widths: V 90 · E 72 · R 82 · D 88 · H 86 · O 91 · U 82.5 · S 74.

The O is **narrower than it is tall** (0.91). The S is drawn as a variable-width offset of
a single centreline — the stroke thins where it runs horizontal and thickens where it runs
vertical, following the same optical rule as O and U — with flat-cut terminals.

### The block

Both lines are set to an **identical measure of 531.5**, so VERDE / HOUSE forms a true
rectangle: flush left, flush right, both lines ending on an E that aligns exactly. Because
the letters differ in width, the tracking differs slightly per line (VERDE 32.32, HOUSE
31.50) — that is the mechanism, not an error.

* Tracking averages **0.319 cap** — about 15 % tighter than the previous wordmark.
* Per-pair optical kerning: VERDE 29.96 / 32.96 / 32.21 / 34.16 · HOUSE 32.55 / 31.95 / 30.45 / 31.05.
* Leading (baseline to baseline) **116** = 1.16 cap, down from 1.35.
* The V's apex hangs **1.8** left of the H stem so the two lines align optically, not mathematically.

## 3 · The lockups

**Primary** — symbol height **330** (3.30 cap), clear channel **62** (0.62 cap) between
symbol and wordmark. The symbol is centred on the two-line block at the midpoint between
its bounding centre and its centre of ink: 53.4 above the VERDE cap line, 60.6 below the
HOUSE baseline.

**Horizontal** — symbol height 186 (1.86 cap), channel 52, word space 1.95 × the letter gap.

## 4 · Clear space

Clear space on every lockup = **the cap height** (100 units) on all four sides. Minimum
sizes: primary lockup 24 mm / 96 px wide; horizontal lockup 32 mm / 128 px wide;
symbol 6 mm / 20 px tall (micro cut).

## 5 · Colour

See `colors.json` / `colors.css`. Four colours, no tints.

| | HEX | RGB | CMYK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verde Green | `#1A3925` | 26 57 37 | 80 / 45 / 75 / 55 |
| Warm White | `#F4F1E9` | 244 241 233 | 3 / 3 / 7 / 0 |
| Black | `#131614` | 19 22 20 | 70 / 55 / 60 / 90 |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 |

CMYK values are press builds, not conversions — proof them before a run. Pantone
candidates to check against a physical guide: 5535 C, 3435 C, 350 C.

Contrast: Verde on Warm White **11.2:1**, Verde on Pure White **12.7:1**,
Black on Warm White **16.1:1**. All pass WCAG AAA.

**Decided.** A second palette was carried through the same tests — `#1F3D2E` /
`#F9F7F2` / `#1A1A1A` — and is not used. It cleared AAA identically, so contrast
never separated them; temperature and geography did. Verde House works out of
Gilbert, Arizona. The shipped green sits at hue 141°, near a nopal pad; the
alternative at 150° is a conifer green, a northern colour on a Sonoran mark. The
shipped warm white carries 4.5 % chroma and reads as plaster against true white,
where the alternative measured 1.07:1 and barely read as warm. And the shipped
black carries a faint green cast, so the one-colour cut stays in the family
rather than going neutral and cold.

For reference, the parent company's greens sit at hue 86–93° — a bright crop
yellow-green. Northwest Ag Technologies and Verde House are deliberately two
different greens, not a near-miss.
