Colour — decided

Both cleared AAA on their own ground, so contrast never decided this. Temperature did, and so did geography: Verde House works out of Gilbert, Arizona. A ships. At hue 141° it sits near a nopal pad; B at 150° is a conifer green — a northern colour on a Sonoran mark. A's warm white carries 4.5 % chroma and reads as plaster next to true white, where B's registers at 1.07:1 and is barely warm at all. And A's black carries a faint green cast, so the one-colour cut stays inside the family instead of going neutral and cold. For the record, the parent's greens sit at 86–93° — a bright crop yellow-green — so parent and subsidiary are deliberately two different greens, not a near-miss.
