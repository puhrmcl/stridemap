How it works

We supply the labor. The program stays yours.

Contract sampling is all we do on the ag side now — crews, not consulting. You keep the protocol, the lab relationship and the recommendations. We put trained people on the acres in the window you have.

  1. 1

    Your program

    You set the protocol — density, depth, zones or grid, the lab it goes to. We don't change it and we don't second-guess it.

  2. 2

    Our crews

    Trained samplers who have done this across nine states. They show up knowing what a clean pull looks like and what a mislabeled bag costs.

  3. 3

    Your window

    Fall is short. We staff to the acres and the days you actually have, alongside your own people or in place of them.
